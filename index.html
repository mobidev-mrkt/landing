FAQ

Questions founders ask before sending code

What exactly do I get for $399?

An experienced developer reviews your full AI-built codebase and runs a tech analysis and a security check. You get a prioritized list of the main quality and security issues, each rated by severity and business impact, a plain-language summary of what's wrong and what it puts at risk, and any compliance red flags we spot. Delivered in one business day.

This tier is the audit itself. Code fixes start at the next package.

What if my app is built on Cursor, Claude Code, Windsurf or GitHub Copilot?

Whichever tool you used, it produced a real codebase underneath, and that codebase is what we work on. To get started, send us your source code, either as an archive (.zip) or a Git repository. Most of these tools let you export it. If you're not sure how, tell us which one you used and we'll walk you through it.

What counts as a production blocker?

Anything stopping your app from working for real users: a broken checkout, a login that fails, a crash on a core flow. That's what the fix packages solve, one or two at a time. If you're not sure whether your issue qualifies, tell us what's happening before you pick a package and we'll confirm.

What happens if the fix doesn't work?

Then you don't pay for it. If the fix we deliver doesn't actually resolve the blocker, tell us within the 48-hour review window and we cancel the payment hold, no charge. We stand behind the fix, not just the diagnosis.

Which stacks and tools do you support?

Any modern web stack. We've reviewed codebases built with Cursor, Claude Code, Windsurf, Lovable, Bolt, Replit, v0, and GitHub Copilot, on top of common stacks like Supabase, Firebase, Next.js and React Native, plus the usual JavaScript/TypeScript, Python, and other frameworks underneath them. If it runs in a browser, on a phone, or as an API, we can audit it.

How do I export my code from Lovable, Bolt or Replit?

Each of these lets you connect a GitHub repo or download a .zip from the project's settings. If you can't find the option, tell us which tool you used and we'll point you to the exact menu.

Will you need access to my production environment?

No. We work from your source code, sent as a .zip or a Git repo. We don't need logins to your hosting, database, or admin panel unless a specific finding requires confirming something live, and we'll always ask first.

What does further work cost after the audit?

Whatever you ask for, scoped and quoted before you pay, the same way this page works: a fixed price and a delivery date, no hourly billing. Most follow-up requests are scoped within a day of your audit report.

How does the money-back guarantee work?

Here is how the 100% Satisfaction Guarantee works:

  • Safe payment hold. When you place an order we don't charge your card. We place a temporary hold (authorization) on the funds.
  • 48-hour review window. Once we deliver your audit report or code fixes, you have 48 hours to review the work.
  • Capture or cancel. Happy with the result? We finalize the payment. Not happy? Tell us within those 48 hours, we cancel the transaction and the hold is released immediately. (If you reject the work and payment is canceled, you may not use, deploy or copy the provided fixes or reports.)
  • Automatic approval. No feedback within the 48-hour window means everything works, and we capture the payment to complete the project.

The guarantee exists so you can order without talking to anyone first. We reserve the right to refuse future work to anyone who makes a habit of abusing it.

What if it needs more than the audit?

Often the audit shows the issue is bigger than one package. That's fine. Every package comes with documented audit results you own, written so you can take them anywhere: use them in-house, hand them to any developer you like, or keep working with us on a clearly scoped basis. No vendor lock-in.

Is my code kept confidential?

Yes. Your code stays yours, and it stays private:

  • Strictly confidential. Only vetted, NDA-bound team members access your codebase, and strictly to deliver your service.
  • Safe AI use. We use secure, professional-tier AI tools to assist with fixes. Your code is never used to train public AI models.
  • Clear ownership. You keep 100% ownership of your original code, and you gain full ownership of our fixes and reports the moment payment is finalized.
