Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026 · MobiDev Corporation (State of Georgia, USA)

This Privacy Policy explains how MobiDev Corporation ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you visit our website and use our "AI Code Rescue / Vibe Coding Fix" services (the "Services"). Please read it carefully. By using our Services, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

1. Information we collect

To provide our Services effectively and securely, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal identification information: name, email address, phone number, and other contact details you provide when requesting our Services.
  • Technical and usage data: IP address, geographical location, browser type, and operating system. We collect this to ensure service stability, verify customer identity (KYC), and prevent fraudulent transactions.
  • Financial information: payment details and billing addresses. All payment processing is securely handled directly by our third-party payment processor, Stripe. We do not store or process your full credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Customer data and codebases: source code, repository links, architectural documentation, and any other input materials, files, or data you share with us for the purpose of receiving our code auditing and refactoring Services.

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect primarily to deliver, maintain, and improve our Services. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Perform code audits, diagnostics, and refactoring as requested.
  • Process transactions and manage the temporary authorization holds on your payment method.
  • Communicate with you regarding your order, technical feedback, and customer support.
  • Verify your identity and prevent fraudulent activities or abuse of our refund policy.

3. How we share your information and third-party services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or Customer Data (including your code) to outside parties. We only share information with trusted third parties under strict confidentiality obligations, and solely to operate our business and deliver the Services:

  • Payment processor: we share necessary billing and contact information with Stripe to process your payments, authorize funds, and conduct fraud verification (for example, Address Verification System).
  • AI infrastructure partners: to provide fast and accurate code fixes, we utilize secure, professional-tier Artificial Intelligence tools. The specific AI services we employ include GitHub Copilot, Google Gemini, OpenAI Codex, and Anthropic Claude. AI privacy guarantee: we only use enterprise or API tiers of these tools. Your code and input materials are processed securely and are never used by these third-party providers to train public AI models.
  • Independent contractors: we may share access to your codebase with our vetted independent software engineers and consultants who assist in delivering the Services. All such personnel are bound by strict Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) and may only access your data for the duration of the project.
  • Legal obligations: we may disclose your information if required to do so by law, in response to a subpoena, or to comply with requests by law enforcement agencies in the State of Georgia or federal authorities of the United States.

4. Data security and confidentiality

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal information and codebase. While we have taken reasonable steps to secure the personal information you provide to us, please be aware that no security measures are perfect or impenetrable. We treat your codebase as highly confidential trade secrets and restrict access on a strict need-to-know basis.

5. Data retention

We will retain your personal information and Customer Data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. Once the Services are completed (or canceled) and the payment is finalized, we will securely delete or destroy our copies of your codebase, except as necessary to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our legal agreements.

6. Your data privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, to request that we correct any inaccuracies, or to request the deletion of your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below.
